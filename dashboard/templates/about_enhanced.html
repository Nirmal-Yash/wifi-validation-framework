WHY NETFORGE

Network validation should be reproducible.

Network engineering often depends on environments that are manually assembled, difficult to reproduce and weakly connected to evidence. NetForge applies software quality-engineering discipline to network firmware validation: define the topology, reproduce the environment, execute the same validation contract, capture what happened, compare it with a baseline and make the release decision from evidence.
