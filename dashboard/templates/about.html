NetForge Validation Platform

An advanced, production-grade Software Quality Assurance architecture engineered to automate rigorous validation testing across complex wireless and routed environments.

Core Mechanics

  • Linux Network Namespaces (`netns`): Isolates daemons and routing logic into independent environments on a single host.
  • mac80211_hwsim Emulation: Instantiates virtual Wi-Fi hardware radios directly in kernel memory, bypassing physical RF hardware requirements.
  • GNS3 Protocol Interop: Parses standard `.gns3` topology schemas natively into `netns` bridges and links.
